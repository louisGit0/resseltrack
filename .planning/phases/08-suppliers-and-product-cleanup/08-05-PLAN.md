<objective>
SUP-02: link orders to a saved supplier, optionally and backward-compatibly. The order form's free-text supplier field becomes a `<select>` of the user's suppliers plus an "Autre" option that reveals the existing free-text input. When a supplier is picked, both `orders.supplier_id` and the resolved supplier name (dual-write, D-02) are stored; picking nothing or "Autre" falls back to free text. Posted ids are re-validated for ownership (IDOR control).

Purpose: Connects the supplier list to orders without breaking any existing order.
Output: Modified `OrderController.php`, `Order.php`, `orders/form.php`, `app.js`.
Depends on Plan 03 (Supplier model `find()`/`allForUser()`) and Plan 04 (owns the prior app.js edit — this plan adds the supplier toggle to the same file).
</objective>
